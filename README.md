# CEED English Path · B2.1

An interactive, self-paced English course adapted to the CEEDCV B2.1 programme for 2026–2027.

## Open the app

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No installation, build step or account is required.

The CEEDCV logo is included locally in `assets/ceed-logo.png`. “Created by Ana H. Iranzo” is visible in the header, and the materials credit remains visible at the bottom of every page.

## Course contents

The app follows the six units, tasks and calendar shared for the course:

1. Music — verb patterns, agreement, connectors, pronunciation and monologue.
2. Food — articles, present perfect, dependent prepositions, phrasal verbs, mediation and review.
3. Politics, history and diversity — narrative tenses, relative clauses, debate and opinion essay.
4. Travel — regrets, correlative conjunctions, inversion and complaint letters.
5. Age and the body — past habits, getting used to, advice, descriptions and oral mediation.
6. Art — passive voice, inversion, adjective gradability and written mediation.

Each unit includes expanded visual explanations in English, tables, examples, common errors, vocabulary flashcards with English definitions, Spanish translations and example sentences, a 20-question interactive practice set, a reading illustrated with its open-licensed unit photo, six comprehension questions, and vocabulary and grammar expressions underlined with Spanish hover/focus translations. The theory and printable study pack each show the unit's credited illustration once, before the topic-by-topic material. The mediation studio includes a topic-specific infographic, a separate oral task for the stated audience, preparation and speaking checklists, a 90-second practice timer and a model answer. It also includes speaking prompts, an illustrated photo-description activity and an exam-style task. Completion and learning activity are stored in the browser's local storage.

On wide screens, the course units appear in a persistent left sidebar. Opening a unit reveals a mini-index of its theory points; selecting a point opens the theory section and scrolls directly to it. Every unit section also shows a visual three-step learning guide. On smaller screens, the unit and topic navigation moves to a compact horizontal menu.

The theory uses emoji-led visual grammar cards, topic-specific emoji illustrations, quick rules, pattern maps, expanded explanations, examples and common-error corrections. The final tab in each unit opens a printable study pack containing the unit theory and topic-grouped practice, vocabulary with Spanish translations, reading and comprehension, speaking prompts, exam task, written and oral mediation activities, and answer keys. Use the print button and select a printer or “Save as PDF”.

The year plan uses the supplied September 2026–June 2027 calendar. Dates for Unit 4 Part 3 and Unit 5 Part 2 are reproduced as shown in the source calendar; the app flags their out-of-sequence placement rather than silently changing them.
