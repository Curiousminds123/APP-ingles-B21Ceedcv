# CEED English Path · B2.1

An interactive, self-paced English course adapted to the CEEDCV B2.1 programme for 2026–2027.

## Open the app

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No installation, build step or account is required.

The CEEDCV logo is included locally in `assets/ceed-logo.png`. “Created by Ana H. Iranzo” is visible in the header, and the materials credit remains visible at the bottom of every page.

## Course contents

The app follows the six units, tasks and calendar shared for the course:

1. Music — verb patterns, agreement, connectors, pronunciation and monologue.
2. Food — articles, present perfect, dependent prepositions, phrasal verbs, mediation and review.
3. Politics, history and diversity — narrative tenses, relative clauses, debate and opinion essay.
4. Travel — regrets, correlative conjunctions, inversion and complaint letters.
5. Age and the body — past habits, getting used to, advice, descriptions and oral mediation.
6. Art — passive voice, inversion, adjective gradability and written mediation.

Each unit includes expanded visual explanations in English, grammar boxes for every point, topic-specific functional-language phrases with Spanish translations, tables, examples, common errors, vocabulary flashcards with English definitions, Spanish translations and example sentences, a 20-question interactive practice set, a reading illustrated with its open-licensed unit photo, six comprehension questions, and vocabulary and grammar expressions underlined with Spanish hover/focus translations. The theory and printable study pack each show the unit's credited illustration once, before the topic-by-topic material. The mediation studio includes a topic-specific infographic, a separate oral task for the stated audience, preparation and speaking checklists, a 90-second practice timer and a model answer. It also includes speaking prompts, an illustrated photo-description activity and an exam-style task.

Vocabulary flashcards use topic-matched emoji illustrations. The Unit 1 pronunciation guide includes listen-and-repeat controls for sample sounds, words and intonation in British and American English. Every reading also has British/American audio playback controls and a stop button. Audio uses browser speech synthesis and the voices installed on the learner's device; available voice choices depend on the browser and operating system. Sound examples distinguish /dʒ/ (jam, major) from /tʃ/ (chart, chair), and /ʒ/ in genre.

The sidebar includes an extra Past verbs lab with a reference table of common irregular verbs, explanations of past simple, past continuous and past perfect, and sentence-completion, word-order and verb-matching practice. My progress keeps unit checklists, manual completion ticks for speaking/writing/photo/mediation, practice and reading scores, and dated attempts for the past-tense activities in the browser's local storage.

The Exam task tab in every unit includes a unit-specific Copilot writing tutor prompt, planning guide and prompt preview. Copy the prompt, open Copilot and paste it into a new chat for step-by-step planning, feedback and revision guidance. The prompts cover a music blog post, restaurant review, opinion essay, complaint letter, advice email and written museum mediation; they include CEED B2.1 practice rubric guidance and distinguish extra writing practice from the official oral tasks in Units 1 and 5.

On wide screens, the course units appear in a persistent left sidebar. Opening a unit reveals a mini-index of its theory points; selecting a point opens the theory section and scrolls directly to it. Every unit section also shows a visual three-step learning guide. On smaller screens, the unit and topic navigation moves to a compact horizontal menu.

The theory uses emoji-led visual grammar cards, topic-specific emoji illustrations, quick rules, pattern maps, expanded explanations, examples and common-error corrections. The final tab in each unit opens a printable study pack containing the unit theory and topic-grouped practice, vocabulary with Spanish translations, reading and comprehension, speaking prompts, exam task, written and oral mediation activities, and answer keys. Use the print button and select a printer or “Save as PDF”.

The year plan uses the supplied September 2026–June 2027 calendar. Dates for Unit 4 Part 3 and Unit 5 Part 2 are reproduced as shown in the source calendar; the app flags their out-of-sequence placement rather than silently changing them.
